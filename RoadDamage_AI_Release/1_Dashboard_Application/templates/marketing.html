DETECTION CAPABILITIES

5 Damage Types

Click any damage type below to see details

Longitudinal Crack

Cracks that run parallel to the pavement's centerline or laydown direction. Often caused by poor joint construction, temperature cycling, or reflective cracking from layers below.

Transverse Crack

Cracks that run perpendicular to the road direction. Usually caused by thermal contraction in cold weather or shrinkage of the asphalt surface layer.

Alligator Crack

Interconnected web-like crack patterns resembling alligator skin. A sign of structural failure caused by repeated traffic loading on weakened pavement layers.

Pothole

Bowl-shaped depressions in the road surface. The most dangerous defect type — potholes form when pavement material breaks away due to water infiltration and traffic wear.

Repair

Previously patched or repaired areas of pavement. These regions indicate maintenance activity and may need monitoring for re-deterioration.
